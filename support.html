Rackstar Support

Need help? Open a ticket at github.com/VeryBarry/rackstar-site/issues (a free GitHub account is needed) and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

I bought something but it isn't showing. Open the ⋯ menu, tap Shop, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Does my progress sync between devices? No. Progress is saved on your device only. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Tap Privacy Options in the Shop (shown where required), or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
